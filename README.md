> (Apache-2.0), originally developed by IceWhale Technology Co., Ltd.
> Building on that foundation, NimoOS adds an AI agent, RAG-based

NimoOS AppStore needs your help to grow:

- See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for how to contribute apps in Docker Compose format.

  **IMPORTANT**: Your PR must be *well tested* on your own NimoOS first. This is the mandatory first step for your submission.

## 3rd-Party AppStores

NimoOS lets you customize your device with a variety of third-party app stores. NimoOS is compatible with app stores in the CasaOS format (the `x-casaos` Docker Compose extension), so the community stores below work out of the box.

Simply copy the provided source link and add it to your NimoOS settings to access the corresponding app store.

- [Awesome CasaOS store list](https://awesome.casaos.io/content/3rd-party-app-stores/list.html)
